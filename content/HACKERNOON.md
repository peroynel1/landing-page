Originally published at https://vlyt.app/blog/send-quote-whatsapp-without-losing-numbers/

WhatsApp is where many customers say yes. It is a bad place to invent prices. The fix is simple: treat WhatsApp as the courier, not the calculator.

## Direct answer

Build the quote in a system that stores line items and totals, then share that finished quote on WhatsApp. Never let the chat become the only copy of the numbers.

## The workflow

1. Pull lines from a price catalog (item, amount, tax note).
2. Structure the quote: lines, total, valid-until, exclusions.
3. Label tax clearly (in South Africa that often means VAT incl/excl).
4. Share on WhatsApp (PDF or a short summary that points at the document).
5. If they negotiate, edit the saved quote, then resend — do not only type a new total in the thread.

## A message template that stays clean

> Hi Thabo — quote for the bathroom redo is attached. Total R12,480 incl VAT, valid until Friday. Reply yes to book the deposit.

Short. Points at the document. States validity.

## What not to do

- Renegotiate across dozens of voice notes with no updated quote.
- Screenshot a half-scrolled spreadsheet.
- Send three different totals without labelling which one is current.

## When spreadsheets make this worse

If you export from Sheets, retype into chat, then discount in chat, you have three truths. The customer remembers the lowest number they saw. You remember the one that still made margin. Chat search remembers everything and clarifies nothing.

## FAQ

### Should I type the quote inside WhatsApp?

No for anything with more than one or two lines. Type once in your quote tool, then share. Chat typing creates version drift.

### PDF or plain text?

Either works if totals are clear. PDF preserves layout; text is faster to skim. Pick what your customers actually open.

### How do I handle “can you do it cheaper?”

Revise the saved quote, then resend. Do not only reply with a new total in the thread.

### What about voice notes?

Fine for relationship. Terrible as the only price record. Follow with the written quote.
