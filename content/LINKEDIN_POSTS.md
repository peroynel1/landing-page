## Post 1 — Checklist (ship first)

COPY FROM NEXT LINE:

WhatsApp is where SA customers say yes. It is a terrible place to store your prices.

If you quote from chat history, you will send the wrong total eventually — usually on the job that mattered.

Before the next quote, run a 2-minute check:
• Catalog prices in ZAR
• VAT labelled
• Valid-until date
• Exclusions stated
• Share from a saved quote (do not retype the total)

Free printable checklist for SA micro-businesses:
https://vlyt.app/resources/sa-quoting-checklist/

## Post 2 — WhatsApp without losing numbers

COPY FROM NEXT LINE:

The quote is not the WhatsApp message. The message is just the envelope.

Build the lines in a system that keeps quantities, VAT and the total. Then share.

If they negotiate, edit the saved quote and resend — do not only type a new number in the thread.

Guide:
https://vlyt.app/blog/send-quote-whatsapp-without-losing-numbers/

## Post 3 — Spreadsheet failure mode

COPY FROM NEXT LINE:

Spreadsheets are fine until you are on site, the customer is waiting, and version 3 of the quote lives in someone’s Downloads folder.

If your source of truth only works on a laptop, you do not have a quoting system — you have a file.
