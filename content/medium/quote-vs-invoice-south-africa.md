# Quote vs invoice in South Africa: when you need which

*Originally published at [https://vlyt.app/blog/quote-vs-invoice-south-africa/](https://vlyt.app/blog/quote-vs-invoice-south-africa/).*

**A quote offers a price. An invoice asks for money.** Mixing the two is one of the fastest ways to confuse a South African customer — and yourself — when you sell through WhatsApp.

This comparison sits under [running your SA business from your phone](https://vlyt.app/blog/run-sa-business-from-phone/).

## Side-by-side

| | Quote | Invoice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | Offer / estimate | Request for payment |
| Customer action | Accept, change or decline | Pay |
| Timing | Before (or while scoping) the job | When billing for supply or agreed work |
| Typical WhatsApp moment | “Here is the price for the job” | “Here are banking details / pay link” |
| Stock impact | Soft hold at most | Often when sale is confirmed |
| VAT | Should be clear on the total | Tax invoice rules if you are a vendor |

## Direct answer

If the customer has not agreed yet, send a **quote**. If they owe you money for what you supplied or contracted, send an **invoice**. Do not label a payment request as a “quote” to sound softer — it creates disputes later.

## What belongs on each

### Quote essentials

Business identity, customer, date, validity, line items, ZAR totals, VAT treatment, exclusions. Full walkthrough: [how to write a professional quote in South Africa](https://vlyt.app/blog/how-to-write-professional-quote-south-africa/).

### Invoice essentials

Everything needed to pay: amount due, payment method, invoice number or reference, and — if you are VAT-registered — the fields SARS expects on a tax invoice. Quoting VAT clearly earlier avoids sticker shock; details in [VAT on quotes](https://vlyt.app/blog/vat-on-quotes-small-business-sa/).

This article is **not** tax advice. Confirm your own SARS obligations with a practitioner if you are unsure.

## Language that keeps chats clean

- Quote: “Valid until Friday. Reply yes to go ahead.”
- Invoice: “Invoice 1042 for the Aug install. Amount due R… Reference …”

If you revise a quote, keep version history. If you revise an invoice, issue a credit note or a clearly marked replacement — do not silently edit a paid document.

## How this fits the phone loop

In a phone-first system you create the quote from a catalog, share it on WhatsApp, then convert or create an invoice when payment is due. Stock and cashflow only make sense when you know which documents are offers and which are money.

## FAQ

### What is a quote?

A quote is an offer to do work or supply goods at a stated price. It is not a demand for payment. The customer can accept, negotiate or decline.

### What is an invoice?

An invoice is a request for payment for goods or services supplied (or agreed). It usually includes payment details and, for VAT vendors, the information a tax invoice needs.

### Can one PDF be both?

Avoid it. Mixing “quote” and “invoice” on the same document confuses customers and messes up your own follow-up. Convert a accepted quote into an invoice when you bill.

### Do I need an invoice for every small job?

If you expect payment and need a paper trail, yes. For tiny COD cash jobs some operators still only quote verbally — that is a risk when amounts are disputed later.

### Where does VAT sit?

Quotes should already show how VAT is treated so the customer is not surprised. Invoices for VAT-registered sellers must follow SARS tax invoice rules. See our VAT-on-quotes guide for the quoting side.

### What should I send on WhatsApp first?

Usually the quote. After acceptance and delivery (or per your deposit rules), send the invoice with payment details.
