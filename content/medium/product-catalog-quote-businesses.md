# Product catalog for quote-based businesses

*Originally published at [https://vlyt.app/blog/product-catalog-quote-businesses/](https://vlyt.app/blog/product-catalog-quote-businesses/).*

If the catalog is wrong, every quote is wrong. For quote-based South African businesses, the catalog is not a nice-to-have web shop — it is the **price memory** of the company.

Part of [running the business from your phone](https://vlyt.app/blog/run-sa-business-from-phone/).

## Direct answer

Build a catalog of the products and services you actually sell, with ZAR prices you trust. Build every quote from those lines. Update the catalog when suppliers change — not only the last WhatsApp message.

## What to store per item

- Clear name (customer language, not only supplier codes)
- Unit (each, m, kg, hour)
- Sell price in ZAR
- Optional cost (for your margin sense)
- Variations / packaging if price or stock differs
- Stock quantity if you hold inventory ([stock guide](https://vlyt.app/blog/stock-tracking-phone-traders/))

## Catalog hygiene rules

1. One item, one meaning — do not create “Cable”, “cable”, and “CAB-6” as three truths.
2. Retire dead items instead of leaving ghost prices.
3. Review top movers monthly when copper, cement or imports move.
4. Never let a one-off chat discount silently become the new list price without a decision.

## From catalog to quote

With a clean list, [writing a professional quote](https://vlyt.app/blog/how-to-write-professional-quote-south-africa/) is assembly, not invention. That is how you stay fast on site.

## Shipping and packaging notes

If you sell packed goods, store pack size on the item so you do not quote single-unit prices for bulk packs by mistake.

Vlyt is built around a catalog you reuse on every quote, with stock visible on items — priced once, quoted many times.

## FAQ

### What is a catalog in a quoting business?

A saved list of what you sell — products or services — with the price and details you reuse on quotes instead of inventing them in chat.

### How many items should I add first?

Start with the 20 you quote most. Perfect taxonomy can wait. Coverage of real work matters more.

### Should services be in the catalog?

Yes. Labour lines and call-out fees belong next to goods so quotes stay consistent.

### What about variations?

Capture size, colour, pack size or grade as variations when the price or stock differs. Do not duplicate whole products without a reason.

### How does stock fit?

Stock levels sit on catalog items. See our stock tracking guide for phone-friendly habits.

### Can I import from a spreadsheet once?

Yes as a one-time migration. After that, edit prices in the catalog so WhatsApp never becomes the master list again.
