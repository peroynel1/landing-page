# Why spreadsheet quoting breaks down (and what to use instead)

*Originally published at [https://vlyt.app/blog/spreadsheet-quoting-problems/](https://vlyt.app/blog/spreadsheet-quoting-problems/).*

Spreadsheets are excellent calculators. They are **poor field systems** for South African sellers who close jobs on WhatsApp between site visits.

This article sits in the [phone-first business pillar](https://vlyt.app/blog/run-sa-business-from-phone/).

## Direct answer

If your quote price lives in a sheet but the customer negotiation lives in chat, you will eventually send a total that neither file nor chat can fully explain. Move the **source of truth** for products and quotes onto a phone-friendly catalog.

## Failure modes we see repeatedly

1. **Version fog** — `Quote_Jan`, `Quote_Jan_fixed`, `Quote_Jan_use_this`.
2. **Chat drift** — discounted in WhatsApp, sheet never updated.
3. **VAT maths by hand** — wrong inclusive total under time pressure.
4. **No stock link** — sheet prices items that are already gone.
5. **Single-laptop dependency** — helper cannot quote while you are on site.

## A calm migration path

1. List the 20 lines you quote most often.
2. Enter them into a catalog with ZAR prices.
3. Rebuild one live quote from that catalog.
4. Stop pasting totals into chat as free text.
5. Keep the old sheet read-only for a month.

## FAQ

### Are spreadsheets bad for quoting?

They are fine for one person at a desk. They get brittle when you quote from a bakkie, revise prices in WhatsApp, and need stock or VAT clarity on the fly.

### What usually breaks first?

Version control. File names like Quote_FINAL_v7 and chat prices that never make it back into the sheet.

### Can I keep Sheets for reports?

Yes. Many operators still export monthly numbers. The day-to-day quote should not depend on opening a laptop.

### When should I switch tools?

When you have sent the wrong price twice, or when stock and quotes disagree, or when only you understand the spreadsheet formulas.

### What should replace it?

A catalog that feeds quotes on your phone, with clear VAT and shareable output. Compare options in our 2026 quoting apps guide.

### Will I lose my history?

Migrate active products and open quotes first. Archive old sheets as reference; do not rebuild every historical tab on day one.
