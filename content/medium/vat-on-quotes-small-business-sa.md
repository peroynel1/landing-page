# VAT on quotes for small SA businesses

*Originally published at [https://vlyt.app/blog/vat-on-quotes-small-business-sa/](https://vlyt.app/blog/vat-on-quotes-small-business-sa/).*

**Customers pay the number they believe they agreed to.** On South African quotes, VAT confusion is one of the main reasons that number changes between “yes” in WhatsApp and payment day.

This guide covers how to present VAT on quotes for small sellers. It is part of [running an SA business from your phone](https://vlyt.app/blog/run-sa-business-from-phone/). It is **not** SARS tax advice.

## Direct answer

On every quote, state clearly whether amounts are **VAT inclusive**, **VAT exclusive**, or **no VAT** (for example if you are not a vendor). Put the payable total in ZAR where the eye lands first.

## Inclusive vs exclusive (practical)

| Approach | Customer sees | Best when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inclusive total | One payable number | Most WhatsApp-first jobs |
| Exclusive + VAT line | Net + VAT + gross | B2B buyers who expect a split |
| No VAT | Price with note you are not charging VAT | Non-vendors — do not invent VAT |

Wrong: sending “R10,000” exclusive in chat, then invoicing R11,500 later without having said VAT applied.

## What belongs on the quote

Follow the structure in [how to write a professional quote](https://vlyt.app/blog/how-to-write-professional-quote-south-africa/), and add:

- Explicit VAT treatment near the total
- If exclusive: VAT amount and gross total
- Validity date (supplier VAT-inclusive costs move)

When you bill, switch to invoice language — [quote vs invoice](https://vlyt.app/blog/quote-vs-invoice-south-africa/).

## Phone-friendly habit

Capture prices in your catalog the way you sell them (usually what the customer pays). Let the quote tool apply VAT consistently instead of recalculating in a chat thread after hours.

Vlyt quotes can carry VAT in ZAR so the number you send is the number they pay — still not a full set of SARS books, but enough clarity for day-to-day quoting.

## FAQ

### Should my quote be VAT inclusive or exclusive?

Choose one approach and label it. Many solo operators show a VAT-inclusive total so the customer sees the amount they will pay. If you negotiate exclusive prices, show VAT as a separate line so there is no surprise.

### Do I need to charge VAT if I am not registered?

Generally you do not charge VAT if you are not a VAT vendor. Do not add a fake VAT line. This is not legal advice — confirm with SARS guidance or your accountant.

### Is a quote the same as a tax invoice?

No. A quote is an offer. A tax invoice has specific requirements for VAT vendors. See quote vs invoice for the timing difference.

### What currency should I use?

Quote in ZAR for local customers. Say so explicitly if you ever quote foreign currency for imports.

### How do apps help?

Tools that apply VAT rules on line items or quote totals reduce arithmetic errors when you send from a phone between jobs.
