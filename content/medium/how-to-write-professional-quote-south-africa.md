# How to write a professional quote in South Africa

*Originally published at [https://vlyt.app/blog/how-to-write-professional-quote-south-africa/](https://vlyt.app/blog/how-to-write-professional-quote-south-africa/).*

A professional quote in South Africa is not a long letter. It is a **clear promise of price** the customer can accept, and you can still defend a week later when supplier costs have moved.

This sits inside the wider loop of [running an SA business from your phone](https://vlyt.app/blog/run-sa-business-from-phone/): catalog first, quote second, stock and cashflow after.

## Start with the catalog, not the chat

Open with the products or services you already sell. If you invent a price under pressure in WhatsApp, you will invent a different one next time.

Minimum fields per line:

- Description the customer understands
- Quantity and unit (each, metre, hour, kit)
- Unit price in ZAR
- Line total

Save those once. Reuse them. That is what separates a quote from a chat paragraph. For catalog structure, see [product catalogs for quote businesses](https://vlyt.app/blog/product-catalog-quote-businesses/).

## The quote skeleton that works on a phone

1. **Header** — business name, phone, email or WhatsApp number.
2. **Customer** — who this is for (person or site name).
3. **Date and validity** — “Valid until …” stops endless price debates.
4. **Line items** — from the catalog, not from memory.
5. **VAT note** — inclusive or exclusive; if registered, show VAT clearly.
6. **Total** — the number they should reply “yes” to.
7. **Next step** — how to accept, deposit rules, or site visit notes.

For VAT detail, read [VAT on quotes for small SA businesses](https://vlyt.app/blog/vat-on-quotes-small-business-sa/). For when to switch language to an invoice, read [quote vs invoice in South Africa](https://vlyt.app/blog/quote-vs-invoice-south-africa/).

## Direct answer: what makes it “professional”

Professional means **reproducible**. Another person on your team (or future you) could rebuild the same total from the same lines. That is why tools that pull from a catalog beat typed chat maths.

## WhatsApp is the envelope, not the ledger

Send the finished quote on WhatsApp — that is how SA customers decide. Keep the structured version in your system so a forward, a screenshot crop, or a “can you do it cheaper?” reply does not erase the original lines.

## Checklist before you hit send

- Every line has a quantity.
- Total matches the sum of lines (plus VAT rules you stated).
- Validity date is on the page.
- Stock-sensitive items were checked if you hold inventory.
- You are not calling it an invoice yet if payment terms and tax invoice rules are not ready.

## Common SA traps

- Quoting “about R8k” with no lines — customers will remember the low end.
- Forgetting that copper, cement or imported parts moved since last month.
- Mixing deposit language on a quote with final-invoice language.
- Sending three revised totals in chat without updating the saved quote.

## Keep it short

If the job needs a site visit, say so. Quote what you can measure now; note exclusions. Ambiguous exclusions create unpaid extras and angry threads.

## FAQ

### What must a South African quote include?

Your business name and contact details, customer name, date, clear line items with quantities and ZAR amounts, whether VAT is included, a total, and how long the quote is valid. Payment terms help even before it becomes an invoice.

### Should the quote show VAT inclusive or exclusive?

Pick one and label it. Many small sellers quote VAT-inclusive totals so the customer sees the amount they will pay. If you are VAT-registered, keep a clear trail of how VAT was calculated.

### How long should a quote stay valid?

For trades and materials that move with suppliers, 7 to 14 days is common. Say the date on the quote so you are not locked into last month’s price.

### Can I send a quote only on WhatsApp?

Yes for delivery — but the numbers should come from a saved catalog or quote document. Do not treat the chat as the only record of the price.

### When does a quote become an invoice?

When the customer accepts and you are billing for payment. See our quote vs invoice guide for the language and paperwork difference.

### Do I need a fancy PDF?

Clarity beats decoration. A clean list of lines, VAT note and total that survives a WhatsApp forward is better than a pretty template with wrong prices.
