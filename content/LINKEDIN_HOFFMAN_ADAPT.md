## Post A — adapted from “biggest mistake / wrong search terms”

Hoffman move: name the mistake → show buyer-intent vs research-intent → give 4 practical moves → one number to check.

**Graphic:** attach `linkedin-whatsapp-not-ledger` (WhatsApp closes the job / chat should not store the price)

COPY:

The biggest mistake I see SA solo operators make when trying to win more jobs is treating WhatsApp like a quoting system.

WhatsApp is where customers decide.
It is not where prices should live.

Messages like “how much for…?” are research.
A clear quote with lines, VAT and a valid-until date is the buyer moment.

Here are 4 moves that actually help:

1/ Keep a short catalog (ZAR), not chat history.
2/ Build the quote once — then share. Do not retype the total.
3/ Write valid-until. Materials move.
4/ Every week list what is still awaiting payment.

One number to watch:
If you send 10 quotes and cannot say which 3 are still open, the leak is process — not marketing.

Free checklist:
https://vlyt.app/resources/sa-quoting-checklist/

(I build Vlyt — phone-first catalog, quotes, stock and cashflow for SA operators.)

END COPY

## Post B — adapted from “Companies: Content is king. But then:”

Hoffman move: quote the common slogan → “But then:” list of contradictions → flip to the real investment → closing line.

**Graphic:** attach `linkedin-whatsapp-is-enough-but-then`

COPY:

Solo operators: “WhatsApp is enough.”

But then:
- Prices live in 14 different chats
- Last week’s total becomes this week’s quote
- Stock is a guess until the customer is waiting
- The month only makes sense on Sunday night

Drop the idea that a messaging app replaces a source of truth.

WhatsApp should deliver the quote.
Your catalog should own the numbers.

Or you can carry on:
- Rewriting amounts from memory
- Losing negotiated prices
- Promising items you do not have
- Wondering where the cash went

Most busy operators regret not separating chat from catalog sooner.

Don’t let that be you.

https://vlyt.app/blog/send-quote-whatsapp-without-losing-numbers/

END COPY
