A professional quote in South Africa is not a long letter. It is a **clear promise of price** the customer can accept, and you can still defend a week later when supplier costs have moved.

This sits inside the wider loop of [running an SA business from your phone](/blog/run-sa-business-from-phone/): catalog first, quote second, stock and cashflow after.

## Start with the catalog, not the chat

Open with the products or services you already sell. If you invent a price under pressure in WhatsApp, you will invent a different one next time.

Minimum fields per line:

- Description the customer understands
- Quantity and unit (each, metre, hour, kit)
- Unit price in ZAR
- Line total

Save those once. Reuse them. That is what separates a quote from a chat paragraph. For catalog structure, see [product catalogs for quote businesses](/blog/product-catalog-quote-businesses/).

## The quote skeleton that works on a phone

1. **Header** — business name, phone, email or WhatsApp number.
2. **Customer** — who this is for (person or site name).
3. **Date and validity** — “Valid until …” stops endless price debates.
4. **Line items** — from the catalog, not from memory.
5. **VAT note** — inclusive or exclusive; if registered, show VAT clearly.
6. **Total** — the number they should reply “yes” to.
7. **Next step** — how to accept, deposit rules, or site visit notes.

For VAT detail, read [VAT on quotes for small SA businesses](/blog/vat-on-quotes-small-business-sa/). For when to switch language to an invoice, read [quote vs invoice in South Africa](/blog/quote-vs-invoice-south-africa/).

## Direct answer: what makes it “professional”

Professional means **reproducible**. Another person on your team (or future you) could rebuild the same total from the same lines. That is why tools that pull from a catalog beat typed chat maths.

## WhatsApp is the envelope, not the ledger

Send the finished quote on WhatsApp — that is how SA customers decide. Keep the structured version in your system so a forward, a screenshot crop, or a “can you do it cheaper?” reply does not erase the original lines.

Practical send flow: [send a quote on WhatsApp without losing the numbers](/blog/send-quote-whatsapp-without-losing-numbers/).

## Checklist before you hit send

- Every line has a quantity.
- Total matches the sum of lines (plus VAT rules you stated).
- Validity date is on the page.
- Stock-sensitive items were checked if you hold inventory.
- You are not calling it an invoice yet if payment terms and tax invoice rules are not ready.

## Common SA traps

- Quoting “about R8k” with no lines — customers will remember the low end.
- Forgetting that copper, cement or imported parts moved since last month.
- Mixing deposit language on a quote with final-invoice language.
- Sending three revised totals in chat without updating the saved quote.

## Keep it short

If the job needs a site visit, say so. Quote what you can measure now; note exclusions. Ambiguous exclusions create unpaid extras and angry threads.
