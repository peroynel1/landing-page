If you send ten quotes and cannot name which three are still open, the leak is **process** — not marketing.

## Direct answer

Keep a short **still open** list: customer, job, total, valid-until, last touch. Follow up once with a clear ask. Then mark **won**, **lost**, or **awaiting payment** — do not leave quotes floating in chat forever.

## Why open quotes go dark

Typical solo-operator pattern in South Africa:

- Quotes leave as WhatsApp PDFs or voice-note totals.
- A week later you remember “someone in Midrand” but not the amount or the validity date.
- You send a soft “just checking in” with no link to the original numbers.
- They remember a lower total from an earlier thread. You remember the one that still made margin.

Marketing did not fail. **Status** failed.

## The still-open list (minimum fields)

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Customer / site | So you know who to ping |
| Job one-liner | Bathroom redo vs fence — not “quote” |
| Total + VAT label | The number you will defend |
| Valid until | Natural follow-up deadline |
| Last touch | When you last messaged or called |
| Status | Open · Won · Lost · Awaiting pay |

If your tool cannot show this, a phone note or sheet with those columns beats nothing — until the sheet becomes the second source of truth ([spreadsheet quoting problems](/blog/spreadsheet-quoting-problems/)).

## A follow-up rhythm that fits trades

1. **Day 0** — Send the saved quote with total + valid-until in the cover message.
2. **Day 2–3** — One follow-up if they requested the quote and went quiet.
3. **Day before valid-until** — “Quote stands until tomorrow; happy to revise if scope changed.”
4. **After valid-until** — Archive as lost, or issue a **new** dated quote if they revive the job.

Do not run a daily “just checking” loop. It trains customers to ignore you and fills chat with noise.

## Message templates (keep them short)

**First nudge**

> Hi Thabo — checking if you still want the bathroom quote (R12,480 incl VAT, valid Friday). Happy to adjust scope if something changed.

**Before it expires**

> Reminder: that quote is valid until tomorrow. Reply yes to book the deposit, or tell me what to change and I’ll send an updated quote.

**Close the loop**

> I’ll archive this quote as not going ahead for now. If the job comes back, I’ll send a fresh quote with today’s prices.

Always point at the **document**, not a new number invented in the thread. If they negotiate, [edit and resend](/blog/send-quote-whatsapp-without-losing-numbers/) — same rule as the first send.

## Open vs awaiting payment

| State | Meaning | Cashflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open | They have not accepted | Not cash — pipeline only |
| Won / accepted | Job is yours; pay may be pending | Track under awaiting payment |
| Lost / archived | Dead for now | Out of the open list |
| Paid | Money in | Income bucket |

Mixing “still deciding” with “still paying” is how you buy materials on hope. See [cashflow basics for trades](/blog/cashflow-basics-trades-sa/) and [quote vs invoice](/blog/quote-vs-invoice-south-africa/).

## Habits that take ten minutes a week

- Every Friday: open the still-open list. Nudge, win, lose, or extend with a **new** quote.
- Cap active opens (for many solos, 5–15 is plenty). New quotes means closing old ones.
- Never follow up from memory of the total — open the saved quote first.
