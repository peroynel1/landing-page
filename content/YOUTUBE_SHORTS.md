## Short 1 — WhatsApp isn’t the price list (ship first)

**Length target:** 35–45s  
**Avatar:** calm, conversational (not salesy). SA-friendly English.  
**Background:** plain / office desk / phone on table — avoid flashy stock.  
**On-screen text:** big keywords only (see cues).  
**End card / description link:** https://vlyt.app/resources/sa-quoting-checklist/

### HeyGen script (paste)

```
Biggest mistake I see with South African solo operators?

Treating WhatsApp as the price list.

Chat closes the job. That’s fine.
But chat should not own the numbers.

Before your next quote, do this:
Pull prices from a list — in rand.
Label VAT.
Add a valid-until date.
Say what’s not included.
Then share the saved quote — don’t retype the total from memory.

If they negotiate, edit the quote and resend.
Don’t only type a new number in the thread.

Free checklist for SA micro-businesses — link in the description.
We’re building Vlyt for this exact workflow.
```
