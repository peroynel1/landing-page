## Post 1 — WhatsApp isn’t the price list (ship first)

Attach: `linkedin-whatsapp-not-ledger.jpg`

COPY:

Biggest SA quoting mistake: treating WhatsApp as your price list.

Chat closes the job. A catalog should own the numbers.

Before your next quote:
• Prices from a list (ZAR)
• VAT labelled
• Valid-until date
• What’s not included
• Share from a saved quote — don’t retype the total from memory

Free checklist for SA micro-businesses:
https://vlyt.app/resources/sa-quoting-checklist/

(We’re building Vlyt — phone-first quotes, catalog and stock for solo operators.)

## Post 2 — open quotes leak

COPY:

If you send 10 quotes and can’t name which 3 are still open, the leak is process — not marketing.

Keep a simple “still open” list. Follow up. Close or archive.
