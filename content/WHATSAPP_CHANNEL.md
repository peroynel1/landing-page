## Tip 1 — post today (same as X / pin-worthy)

Biggest SA quoting mistake: treating WhatsApp as your price list.

Chat closes the job. A catalog should own the numbers.

Checklist: https://vlyt.app/resources/sa-quoting-checklist/

## Tip 2

If you send 10 quotes and can’t name which 3 are still open, the leak is process — not marketing.

---

## Tip 3

WhatsApp closes the job.
It should not store the price.

Build the quote once. Then share. If they negotiate, edit and resend — don’t only type a new total in chat.

https://vlyt.app/blog/send-quote-whatsapp-without-losing-numbers/

---

## Tip 4

Before you send any quote:
• ZAR prices from a list
• VAT labelled
• Valid-until date
• What’s not included

https://vlyt.app/resources/sa-quoting-checklist/

---

## Tip 5

Every week, three numbers:
1) Income received
2) Expenses paid
3) Still awaiting payment

Open WhatsApp “yes” is not cash until paid.

https://vlyt.app/blog/cashflow-basics-trades-sa/
