# Ask-community questions — Vlyt research (no product pitch)

Use on: r/askSouthAfrica, r/southafrica, r/smallbusiness, r/SweatyStartup, r/freelance (fit the sub).  
**Rule:** ask about *their* process. Do **not** mention Vlyt, “my app,” or “would you download.” Soft follow-up only if someone asks what you use.

Cadence: **1 question / week** max across all ask communities. Don’t cross-post identical text same day.

## Q1 — Where do prices live?

**Title:**
```
SA freelancers / tradies: where do you actually keep your prices — WhatsApp, Excel, or something else?
```

**Body:**
```
Curious how solo operators here handle this.

When a customer asks “how much for X?” on WhatsApp, where does the number come from?
• Scroll old chats
• Excel / Google Sheet
• Notes app
• Printed price list
• Something else

What’s the most annoying part of that setup when they negotiate or ask again next month?
```

## Q3 — Negotiate on WhatsApp

**Title:**
```
When a customer negotiates on WhatsApp, do you edit a real quote or just type a new total in the chat?
```

**Body:**
```
Honest question for SA / WhatsApp-heavy businesses:

Customer says “can you do it for less?” in the thread.

Do you:
1. Edit a saved quote / PDF and resend, or
2. Just reply with a new number in the chat?

If you do (2), have you ever lost track of which price you actually agreed?
```

## Q4 — Valid-until

**Title:**
```
Do you put an expiry / “valid until” date on quotes? Does anyone care?
```

**Body:**
```
Materials and exchange rates move. Some people put “valid until” on every quote; others never bother.

If you quote regularly:
• Do you use a valid-until date?
• Do customers push back?
• Have you been burned by an old price coming back weeks later?

Genuinely curious what’s normal in your trade.
```
