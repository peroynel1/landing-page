## Create the newsletter (LinkedIn UI)

**Creator mode is gone** (LinkedIn removed the profile toggle in 2024). You do not need it.

1. Open LinkedIn in a **desktop** browser (Home feed).
2. In **Start a post**, click **Write article** (not a normal post).
3. In the article editor, open the **Manage** dropdown (top right of the editor).
4. Click **Create newsletter**.
5. Fill in:
   - **Name:** `Vlyt Tips` (or `Phone-first quoting for SA`)
   - **Description:** paste **Newsletter description** below
   - **Frequency:** Weekly
   - **Logo / cover** (optional): black + mint / app screenshot
6. Save / Done — you now have a newsletter.
7. Paste **Issue 1** into the article body, set the title, **Publish**.
8. Copy the newsletter/article URL → reply here or:
   ```bash
   npm run ops:complete -- --id li-newsletter-setup --url "https://www.linkedin.com/…"
   ```
9. Optional same day or next LI slot: short feed post (**Announce post** below).

**If “Create newsletter” is missing under Manage:**  
Publish Issue 1 as a normal **Article** titled `Vlyt Tips #1 — Don’t treat WhatsApp as your price list`. Newsletter often unlocks after a few articles; keep using the same series title.

## Issue 1 — body (paste into LinkedIn Article / Newsletter editor)

Plain text. LinkedIn won’t render Markdown — use short paragraphs and bullets.

COPY FROM NEXT LINE:

WhatsApp is where SA customers say yes. It is a terrible place to store your prices.

If you quote from chat history, you will send the wrong total eventually — usually on the job that mattered.

Three habits that fix most of the leak:

1. Keep a simple catalog (even a notes list at first): item, ZAR price, VAT incl/excl.
2. Build the quote once, then share. When they negotiate, edit and resend — don’t only type a new total in the thread.
3. Put a valid-until date on every quote. Materials move.

Before your next send, run this 2-minute check:
• Prices from a list
• VAT labelled
• Valid-until date
• What’s not included
• Share from a saved quote, not memory

Free printable checklist:
https://vlyt.app/resources/sa-quoting-checklist/

I’m building Vlyt — phone-first quotes, catalog and stock for SA solo operators. This newsletter is the short version of what we publish on the site. One tip a week, no noise.

If this was useful, subscribe (bell on this newsletter) and share it with one operator who’s still scrolling chat for last week’s price.
