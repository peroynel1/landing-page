## Comment A — no link (use most often)

COPY:

WhatsApp is fine for closing the job. It is a bad place to store prices.

If you quote from chat history you will eventually send the wrong total. Keep a simple catalog (even a notes list): item, ZAR price, VAT incl/excl. When they negotiate, update that list and resend — don’t only type a new number in the thread.

Also put “valid until” on every quote. Materials move.

END COPY

---

## Comment B — soft checklist (1× / day max)

COPY:

Same problem a lot of SA solo operators hit: customer says yes on WhatsApp, but the numbers live nowhere solid.

Before you send:
• Prices in a list (ZAR)
• VAT labelled
• Valid-until date
• What’s not included
• Share from a saved quote, not memory

Free checklist if useful:
https://vlyt.app/resources/sa-quoting-checklist/

(I work on Vlyt — phone quoting for SA small businesses. Disclosure.)

END COPY

---

## Comment C — cashflow

COPY:

Profit on a job is not the same as cash in the account.

Each week know three numbers: income received, expenses paid, and still awaiting payment. Open WhatsApp “yes” is not cash until paid.

END COPY

(Add https://vlyt.app/blog/cashflow-basics-trades-sa/ only if they ask for a write-up.)
