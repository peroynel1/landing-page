```
Building a phone-first quoting app for SA operators who live in WhatsApp
```

**Body:**
```
I’m building Vlyt (vlyt.app) for South African solo operators — plumbers, electricians, small traders — who close jobs on WhatsApp but lose prices in chat history.

The product loop: catalog (ZAR + VAT) → quote → share → stock + simple cashflow. Android. Free while we grow.

What I’m learning so far:
• WhatsApp is the close channel, not the ledger
• “Valid until” on every quote beats arguing about last week’s total
• If you can’t name which of 10 quotes are still open, the leak is process

Curious if anyone here sells into emerging-market / WhatsApp-first SMBs — what’s worked for distribution before the app store listing is live?

Soft leave-behind if useful: free SA quoting checklist → https://vlyt.app/resources/sa-quoting-checklist/
```
