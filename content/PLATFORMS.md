# Platform pool — Vlyt distribution

Canonical content always lives on **vlyt.app**. Other platforms amplify; they do not replace the blog.

**Paused until Play Store:** listicle outreach, Product Hunt–style launches that need a store badge, paid directories that ask for install links.

**Blocked:** Quora (account banned — appeal only; no socks).
